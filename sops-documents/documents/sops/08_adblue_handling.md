# AdBlue (DEF) handling and low-level warnings

Document: SOP-08 · Owner: Fleet Operations · Applies to: all trucks with SCR · Revision: 2

## Purpose
Keep AdBlue (diesel exhaust fluid, DEF) topped up and uncontaminated, to avoid emissions warnings and speed inducement.

## When this applies
- AdBlue level tell-tale on the dashboard
- Fault code SPN 1761 (DEF Tank Level) FMI 17
- Telematics AdBlue level below 10%

## Symptoms and fault codes
| Code | Meaning | Lamp | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPN 1761 FMI 17 | AdBlue tank level low | MIL | Refill at the next stop |

SPN 1761 FMI 17 is an operational alert, not a defect. It is handled by the driver and does not need a workshop visit.

## What the driver should do
1. Top up AdBlue whenever you refuel and the tank is below about 30%. Do not wait for the warning.
2. Use only AdBlue meeting ISO 22241 (32.5% urea). Never top up with water or other fluids.
3. Keep the filler cap clean and closed. Dirt in the tank causes SCR faults later.
4. If the warning stays on after refilling, report it (it may be a level sensor fault).

## What the workshop should do
1. Only if the warning persists with a full tank: check the level sensor and wiring.
2. If AdBlue contamination is suspected (white crystals, low SCR efficiency), test with a refractometer and flush the tank if needed.

## Escalation
- A truck that repeatedly runs low on AdBlue: remind the driver of the refill routine.
- If the tank runs empty, the truck will enter speed inducement (see SOP-03).

## Notes
AdBlue freezes at about -11 °C. The tank and lines are heated and the truck can start normally; the system thaws automatically after start-up.
