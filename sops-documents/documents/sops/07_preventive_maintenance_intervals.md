# Preventive maintenance intervals by duty cycle

Document: SOP-07 · Owner: Fleet Maintenance · Applies to: all trucks · Revision: 3

## Purpose
Define when each truck is due for scheduled service and what the service includes.

## When this applies
Every truck in the fleet. Telematics reports the remaining service distance (serviceDistance) for each truck.

## Symptoms and fault codes
Not fault-driven. A truck is due for service when its remaining service distance reaches 2,000 km or less.

| Duty cycle | Typical models | Service interval |
|---|---|---|
| Long haul | FH, FH16, VNL | every 90,000 km |
| Regional | FM, FMX, VNR | every 60,000 km |
| Urban | FL, FE | every 40,000 km |

## What the driver should do
1. When the display shows service due, inform dispatch so the truck can be booked.
2. Report any defects found on the daily walk-around check (tyres, lights, leaks, windscreen).

## What the workshop should do
At every scheduled service:
1. Change engine oil and oil filters.
2. Change fuel filters, drain the water separator.
3. Lubricate the chassis.
4. Inspect brakes, tyres, steering, suspension, lights and the cooling system.
5. Read fault codes and note any recurring ones.
6. Reset the service distance.

Additional checks:
- October service: battery load test before winter (see SOP-04).
- Urban trucks: check DPF soot load and regeneration history at every service (see SOP-02).

## Escalation
- A truck more than 5,000 km overdue for service is taken off the road until serviced.

## Notes
Planned services take about half a day. Book them on non-working days where possible to avoid lost operating time.
